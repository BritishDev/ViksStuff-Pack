Derived reveal: first 14 seconds after slowing the source to 82 percent pitch/speed, mono conversion, low bass emphasis, high/low pass filters, normalization and short fades.
Derived hum: excerpt starting at source second 4, with the same processing, trimmed to 7.5 seconds and faded for periodic playback.
Rebuild: node scripts/build-orb-sound.mjs (requires FFmpeg). Also invoked by build-orb-assets.mjs.
